## Spend less on the hard parts

**Settings → Expert**, off by default. Nothing is spawned and nothing is spent until you turn
it on.

A cheaper everyday model does the work and consults **Claude** — through your own Claude CLI —
for the parts that need it: planning a change across several files, diagnosing a bug it has
already failed to fix, choosing between designs.

- **Junior mode**, in the chat header, is the arrangement in full: the expert splits the work
  into checkpoints and reviews each one as it lands.
- Consultations continue **one conversation**, which measured about nineteen times cheaper than
  starting cold each time.
- The expert **estimates what the task will cost** when it gives you the plan.
- Set a **budget per conversation** from the header — so many dollars, or so many consultations.
  When it runs out the assistant finishes on its own rather than waiting.
- It can **assess your cheaper model**: five short probes, graded on the real answers, so its
  plans are sized to what that model can actually do.

Every consultation's cost appears in the chat as it happens.
